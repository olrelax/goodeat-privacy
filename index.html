Privacy Policy — Food Logger

Effective date: August 3, 2026

Food Logger: Calories & Cost ("the App", "we", "us") is a personal nutrition, activity, and food-spending tracker. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles your information. Our core principle is simple: your data stays on your devices and in your own iCloud account. We do not collect, sell, or share your personal data.

Data We Collect

We do not collect any personal data. The developer has no servers and no access to your content. The App has no analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking SDKs.

All information you enter — food and activity entries, body metrics (such as weight), prices, catalog items, templates, and settings — is stored:

  • Locally on your device, and
  • In your private iCloud account (via Apple's CloudKit), if you are signed in to iCloud and iCloud sync is enabled.

Data stored in your private iCloud database is accessible only to you. The developer cannot read, access, or retrieve it. iCloud data is handled under Apple's Privacy Policy.

Device Permissions

The App requests the following permissions only to provide specific features. Access is used solely on your request, and the resulting data is stored as described above.

  • Camera — to scan nutrition labels and text. Images are processed on your device and are not uploaded to us.
  • Microphone & Speech Recognition — to let you add entries by voice. Voice input is processed entirely on your device using Apple's on-device Speech Recognition; your audio never leaves your device and is never sent to any server. If on-device recognition is not available for your language on your device, voice input is disabled rather than sending audio elsewhere. We (the developer) never receive or store your audio.

Network Connections

The App can download publicly available reference price data from the official Russian statistics service (rosstat.gov.ru). This is a one-way download of public information. No personal data is transmitted during this request.

Aside from Apple's iCloud/Speech services and this public price data download, the App does not send your information to any external server.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment information. Purchase and subscription handling is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Data Retention and Deletion

Because your data lives on your device and in your private iCloud account, you are in full control:

  • Delete individual entries within the App at any time.
  • Delete the App to remove locally stored data from that device.
  • Manage or delete iCloud data via Settings → [your name] → iCloud on your Apple device.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any data from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Effective date" above.
